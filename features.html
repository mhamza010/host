Platform Features

Tempo empowers corporate housing and vocation rental property managers to achieve financial growth and a competitive edge with an all-in-one platform that simplfies operations and enhance team effieciency. By streamlining reservations, automating routine tasks, and integrating with leading industry platforms, Tempo reduces costs, frees up time and enusres superior guests experience helping you scale effortlessly while staying in control of your business.

Comprehensive Property Management System (PMS)

Oversee all aspects of your property operations, including reservations, housekeeping, maintenance and owner relations, through our intuitive PMS interface. Given full control and insights into your property's performance.

Channel Management to Expand Your Reach

Seamless list and synchronize your properties across multiple platform such as Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and over 60 other channels. Our integrations ensure real-time updates, reducing the risk of double bookings and expanding your property's visibility to a global audienece.

Sell 24x7 with a Direct Booking Website

Create a personlized, Seo-optimized website to accept direct bookings, reducing reliance on third-party platforms and saving on commission fees. Build your brand identity and faster direct relationships with guests.

Mobile Management Apps

Stay connected and manage your properties on the go with our mobile app. Access reservations, communicate with guests, and monitor tasks anytime, anywhere, ensuring flexibility and responsive.

Unified Inbox to Streamline Communications

Stay connected with guests through multiple communication channels in a single, organized inbox. This feature enables prompt responses, fasters better guests relationships, and ensures no message goes unnoticed.

Automation Tools to Boost Efficiency

Automate routine tasks such as booking confirmations, payment reminders, and guests check-ins instructions. By reducing manual workload, you can focus more on delievering exceptional guests experience.

Owner Portal for Transparent Reporting

Provide property owners with access to a dedicated portal where they can view booking calenders, financial reports, and property performace metrics, fostering trust and transparency.

Revenue Management and Dynamic Pricing

Utilize advanced analytics and dynamic pricing tools to optimize your rates based on market demand, seasonality, and competitor analysis, ensuring maximum occupancy and revenue.

Integrated Payment Processing

Simplify transactions with secure, integrated payment solutions that handle multiple currencies and payment methods, providing convenience for both you and your guests.
